We Have Returned.

Our education system teaches the brightest minds to escape the village. We went to IIT Delhi, we saw the world, and we realized a painful truth: No one is building for the 90% of India that lives in villages.

True nation-building cannot happen through mobile apps alone—it requires deep cultural roots, individuals devoted from the heart, and physical infrastructure. We returned to Raghopur to build for Real Bharat.

“

"In the following pages, you will find specific, tangible ways to contribute. Whether you sponsor a ₹5 Lakh classroom, or donate ₹100 for a sapling—we welcome every single rupee. Our Gurukulam will not just be school, we ask you to join us in building a civilizational model."

Shri Mukund Agrawal

IIT Delhi Alumnus

Founder, Divya Bihar Mission

OUR IDENTITY

परिचय

Who We Are.

Divya Bihar Mission is a registered public charitable trust based in Raghopur, Supaul, Bihar, established by a collective of IIT alumni and grassroots leaders.

We operate on a profound conviction: true national progress is impossible if we abandon our villages. We are building a replicable, self-reliant civilizational model across five interconnected pillars.

DBG Gurukulam acts as the physical epicenter of this mission. It is from this school that our vision scales to the community.
